Our story

Built to Give Back

Most businesses don't have a software problem. They have a connection problem. Your apps don't talk to each other. Messages are scattered across email, iMessage, WhatsApp and more, and hours disappear copying the same information from one screen to another.

Tru Integrations was founded on a simple belief: time is the one resource no one can make more of, so our goal is to give some of it back. We take the time to understand how your business really runs, then build automations and integrations that fit, so your tools finally work together and your hours go where they count the most.

No technical language, no unnecessary complexities, no messy software. Just a simple solution to save time and reduce errors.
